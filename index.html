Build YourPitch

Turn Your Story Into Opportunities

1

Who am I?

Give your listener a quick point of orientation.

Include what you're studying, doing, or preparing for and your field or direction.

Example: Psychology student interested in counseling and social work
2

What value do I offer?

What are you especially good at helping people, teams, customers, or organizations do?

Think beyond adjectives. Describe the contribution you make.

Example: I listen carefully, help people clarify what they're facing, and help them think through practical next steps.
3

To whom / toward what outcome?

Where do you want to contribute—and what would you like this conversation to lead to?

Include the type of organization, people, or opportunity you're seeking.

Example: I'm looking for an opportunity to apply and develop these skills in a mental-health counseling setting.

Tips for Practice

  • Speak like you're starting a conversation.
  • Make eye contact and smile.
  • Keep it natural, confident, and concise.
